# Privacy Policy for Zen Fog

**Last Updated: April 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Zen Fog ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and stores your information when you use our macOS application.

By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Zen Fog is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with external servers or third parties.

### A. Data Stored Locally

Zen Fog stores the following data locally on your Mac using macOS's standard UserDefaults:

* **First launch date:** Used to calculate your trial period.
* **Reminder counts** (today and all-time): Used to display your usage stats in the app.
* **App settings:** Your chosen interval, duration, fog effect, language, and other preferences.

This data never leaves your device.

## 2. Purchases

If you choose to unlock Zen Fog, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the Mac App Store. Zen Fog does not process payments, does not receive your payment information, and does not store any purchase-related data itself. All billing is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 3. Third-Party Services

Zen Fog does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services. The only external interaction is with the Mac App Store for purchase verification, which is handled by Apple.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

* **Local Storage:** All app data is stored on your device and is subject to macOS's built-in security and sandboxing protections.
* **No Cloud Storage:** Zen Fog does not upload or sync any data to external servers.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Zen Fog does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
